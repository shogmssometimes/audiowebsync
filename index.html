How this works
  • Every open browser syncs its clock against Firebase's server clock (.info/serverTimeOffset), correcting for local clock drift.
  • Clicking Arm Countdown on any device (the "director") computes a future target instant and broadcasts just that timestamp — not a "play now" signal.
  • Every connected browser independently counts down to that same absolute instant using its own synced clock, and schedules the tone against the Web Audio hardware clock ahead of time.
  • Because the trigger is a future timestamp, normal network latency in delivering it (well under the 10s window) does not affect when the tone actually plays.
  • Sound is mandatory and unlocks automatically on your first tap/click/keypress anywhere on the page (required once per device by browser autoplay rules) — then have one person click "Arm Countdown".
  • When the tone fires, each device shows the exact synced wall-clock time it fired at — compare that timecode across devices to confirm they're lined up.
